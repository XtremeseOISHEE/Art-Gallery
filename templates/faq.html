❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🎨 How do I upload artwork?

Go to the "Upload" button in the navigation bar and fill in the form with your artwork details.

💬 How can I contact the seller?

Click on the "Message Seller" button on the artwork detail page to start a conversation.

🖼️ What file types are supported?

We support JPEG, PNG, and WEBP formats for image uploads.

🔒 Is my information secure?

Yes, we use secure protocols and encryption to protect your personal data.

📦 How do I track my orders?

Go to your profile and select "My Orders" to view status and details of past and current orders.

💸 What payment methods are accepted?

We accept bKash, PayPal, Credit/Debit Cards, and Cash on Delivery.
